# You’re invited to Daily Diary

Open the app: **https://harry934.github.io/Daily-Diary-Management-System/**

## Create an account

Choose **Create an account** and enter:

- Full name
- Username (3–24 characters; letters, numbers, dots, or underscores)
- Organisation name
- Email (optional)
- Password and confirm password (at least 8 characters)
- Programme start date

An admin must approve you before you can sign in. Sign in with your **username** and password.

## Log your days

From your programme start date onward, set time in, time out, and your assignment. Hours are calculated automatically. If you leave before noon, add a short reason.

After you save, the form shows **Done for today**. Use **Edit** if you need to change that day.

## Summary and PDF

Open **Summary** to review every week. Use **Download PDF** in the top bar for a branded report of your logged days and task totals.

## Tasks

Add tasks, set priority (Urgent / High / Medium / Low), and an optional due date. After sign-in, a popup lists urgent or due-soon work.

## Change your password

Open the profile menu (top right) → **Change password**.
