Install Tiyo

Put your internship diary on your home screen. It takes about a minute.

iPhone or iPad

You must use Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iPhone can't install apps.

  1. Open Safari and go to harry934.github.io/Daily-Diary-Management-System.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iOS versions, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Make sure Open as Web App is on (if you see it), then tap Add.
  5. Close Safari and open Tiyo from your home screen.
  6. Sign in or create an account. Tick Keep me signed in so you stay logged in.

PDF reports open in a preview. Tap Share > Save to Files to keep them.

Open Tiyo in Safari

Option A: Install from Chrome

Recommended. It always stays up to date.

  1. Open Chrome and go to harry934.github.io/Daily-Diary-Management-System.
  2. Tap Install app when it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen > Install.
  3. Open Tiyo from your home screen or app drawer.
  4. Sign in or create an account. Tick Keep me signed in.

Option B: Download the APK

Use this if Chrome doesn't offer to install.

  1. Tap Download Tiyo.apk below.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the file to open it.
  3. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Windows, Mac or Chromebook

Works in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

  1. Open harry934.github.io/Daily-Diary-Management-System in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow).
  3. Click Install. Tiyo opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu or Dock.

You can also just bookmark the link and use it in the browser.

Having trouble?

I don't see "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone

Make sure you are in Safari, not Chrome or an in-app browser (like WhatsApp or Instagram). In those apps, tap the menu and choose Open in Safari first.

The app shows an old version or looks wrong

Close Tiyo completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. If it still looks wrong, wait a few minutes and try once more.

I keep getting signed out

Tick Keep me signed in when you log in, and always open Tiyo from the home screen icon, not from a browser tab.

It says I'm offline

Tiyo needs an internet connection. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data, then tap Try again.
