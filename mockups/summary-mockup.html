How this solves your problem

  1. You choose the deadline (Week 9, programme end, or another week). Not hard coded for one person.
  2. No “predicted from your average” as the main answer. Irregular days make that misleading.
  3. Clear math: hours still needed ÷ work days left until your deadline = hours you must do per work day.
  4. Missed days matter: shows how many planned work days you skipped, and a control for “extra days I may miss”.
  5. Optional what if: raise daily hours, or add more misses, and see the new required pace.
  6. Phone: deadline check + Report for AI sit on the Week screen.
