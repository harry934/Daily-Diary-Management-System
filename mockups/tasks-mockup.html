Tasks redesign: mockup

Sample data only. Everything on the phone is clickable.

1
A task is a title with subtasksYou can't save a task without at least one subtask. The task itself has no tick box.
2
Tick subtasks right in the listEvery subtask has its own circle on the card, so you never have to open anything to make progress.
3
The task finishes itselfTicking the last subtask completes the task and moves it to Completed. You get 5 seconds to undo.
4
Progress at a glanceEach card shows the due date, a progress bar and "2/3". Ticked subtasks fold away under "Show 1 done".
5
One button to add"New task" opens one form: title, a quick due date, and subtasks. Press Enter to add the next subtask.
6
Edit and delete in one placeThe ⋯ button opens the same form to rename, change the date, add or remove subtasks, or delete. The last subtask can't be removed.
7
Active and Completed tabsActive is grouped into Overdue, Today, This week and Later. Unticking a subtask on a completed task moves it back to Active.
Try thisTick "Run seed script" to finish a task and see the undo bar. Tap New task and try saving without a subtask. Tap ⋯ on a card to edit it.
